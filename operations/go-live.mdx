## Production activation

1. Complete security and architecture review.
2. Confirm production chains, assets, contract addresses, and signing domains.
3. Create new production credentials with least privilege.
4. Configure production webhook endpoints and verify signatures.
5. Confirm fee policy, user disclosure, revenue recipient, and payout schedule.
6. Execute the full flow with a controlled small amount.
7. Enable monitoring and on-call escalation before opening access.

## Required monitoring

- API availability, latency, and error rate;
- WebSocket connection, sequence gaps, and event lag;
- order rejection and reconciliation breaks;
- margin and liquidation anomalies;
- deposit, withdrawal, and settlement duration;
- webhook delivery failure;
- Builder revenue and payout discrepancies.

## Launch evidence

Retain the identifiers and screenshots or logs for the first production registration, deposit, fill, fee, settlement, withdrawal, revenue entry, and payout. Remove secrets and full signatures from retained evidence.
