---
title: "System status and maintenance"
description: "Protect users and orders during degraded service and maintenance."
---

The status response should report each surface independently:

- public market data;
- private account data;
- order entry and cancellation;
- deposits;
- withdrawals;
- settlement;
- chain and bridge connectivity;
- Builder-filtered streams.

## During maintenance

- Display a clear maintenance state.
- Disable actions that the backend cannot safely accept.
- Keep cancellation available whenever the platform supports it.
- Do not assume existing open orders are automatically cancelled.
- Warn users that positions remain exposed to market and liquidation risk.
- Refresh all account, position, order, and market state before re-enabling trading.

<Warning>
  Private data may be temporarily unavailable during maintenance. Do not replace unknown balances or positions with zero.
</Warning>
