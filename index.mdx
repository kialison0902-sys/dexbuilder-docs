DexBuilder is infrastructure for teams building decentralized trading products. It brings together market access, order execution, accounts, asset flows, settlement, and Builder operations so a team can launch a branded trading experience without assembling each layer independently.

## One platform, multiple market experiences

DexBuilder supports three product families: perpetual DEXs, prediction markets, and event contracts. Each product can be presented through a custom frontend and connected to the platform through documented APIs and integration workflows.

## Built for professional trading products

DexBuilder combines a familiar order-book experience with the account, asset, and operational capabilities required to run a production trading application.

- **Trading infrastructure:** market data, order management, positions, margin modes, funding, liquidation, and trade history.
- **Verifiable account records:** balances, orders, fills, positions, and account-ledger changes are exposed through consistent account interfaces.
- **Flexible product design:** teams control their brand, user experience, market presentation, and integration path.
- **Builder operations:** user onboarding, fee configuration, campaigns, referrals, user data, and revenue settlement are organized as product workflows.
- **Developer-first integration:** bilingual guides, OpenAPI definitions, request signing, examples, WebSocket guidance, and production checklists are maintained in one documentation set.

## How the platform fits together

<Frame caption="DexBuilder platform architecture">
  <img src="/images/platform-architecture.svg" alt="Trading applications connect wallets and users to DexBuilder market, account, ledger, and settlement services" />
</Frame>

A user interacts with a trading application and authorizes account or asset actions. The application consumes public market data and authenticated account APIs, while DexBuilder coordinates execution, position accounting, ledger updates, and settlement. Builder operations provide the project-level controls needed to run the resulting product.
