---
title: "Event contracts"
description: "Builder support for event-based contracts with explicit lifecycle and settlement outcomes."
---

DexBuilder adds event contracts to the market products that a Builder can offer through a branded application.

## Released capability

- present event-based contracts with defined terms and outcome conditions;
- communicate trading availability and lifecycle state throughout the event;
- connect user activity to the platform's account and asset workflows;
- publish the final event outcome and reflect the corresponding settlement result in the user experience.

## Product requirement

An event contract must define the referenced event, the available outcomes, the relevant time window, the resolution rule, and the settlement condition before the contract becomes available to users.
