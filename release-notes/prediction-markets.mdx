---

DexBuilder adds prediction markets to the products that a Builder can launch and present through a custom trading experience.

## Released capability

- create a dedicated prediction-market product experience under the Builder's brand;
- present market questions, possible outcomes, market state, key dates, and resolution results;
- connect the product to the platform's account, asset, and operational workflows;
- maintain a consistent user journey across market discovery, participation, account history, and settlement results.

## Product requirement

Every prediction market must present a clear question, unambiguous outcome definitions, the source or rule used to resolve the market, and the conditions that determine the final settlement result.
