description: "Handle risk states and user messaging when margin becomes insufficient."

## Liquidation

When an account or isolated position no longer satisfies its maintenance-margin requirement, the protocol applies its documented margin and liquidation rules. This may cancel open orders and liquidate part or all of the position.

Clients should display returned values for:

- maintenance margin and margin ratio;
- mark price and estimated liquidation price;
- liquidation status and quantity;
- liquidation fee and resulting balance;
- any remaining position after partial liquidation.

## Insurance fund

The insurance fund helps absorb eligible losses that cannot be covered by the liquidated account. Market-level insurance-fund history is operational information; it is not a substitute for the user's liquidation and ledger records.

## Auto-deleveraging

If liquidation and insurance mechanisms cannot fully absorb a deficit, auto-deleveraging may reduce opposing profitable positions according to the platform ranking rules. Consume the ADL history endpoint and show the executed price, quantity, time, and affected position.

<Warning>
  Never calculate a guaranteed liquidation price in the client. Orders, funding, fees, collateral value, market movement, and risk-tier changes can move the actual trigger.
</Warning>
