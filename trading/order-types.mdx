DexBuilder supports the order controls required by perpetual trading interfaces. Always read contract metadata and the order rules returned for the selected market before submission.

## Core order types

| Order type | Behavior | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Market | Executes immediately against available liquidity | Enter or exit when execution is more important than exact price |
| Limit | Executes only at the limit price or better | Control the worst acceptable execution price |
| BBO | Places relative to a selected best-bid or best-ask level | Join or cross the current book without calculating a price locally |
| Trigger order | Activates when the configured trigger condition is met | Stop loss, take profit, or conditional entry when enabled |

## Time in force

- `GTC` — remains open until filled or cancelled.
- `IOC` — fills immediately as much as possible and cancels the remainder.
- `FOK` — fills the full quantity immediately or cancels the entire order.
- Post-only — may add liquidity but must not execute immediately as a taker.

## Position controls

- **Reduce-only** prevents an order from increasing or reversing the applicable position.
- In hedge mode, specify the position side required by the endpoint.
- For a close action, calculate size from current authoritative position data and handle fills that occur while the request is in flight.

<Warning>
  A market order controls urgency, not price. Display estimated slippage and available depth before submitting a large order.
</Warning>
