---
title: "Positions and margin"
description: "Read positions and manage leverage, margin, and position mode."
---

The current perpetual interface supports:

- position list and individual position queries;
- leverage updates;
- position-margin updates;
- cross and isolated margin modes;
- one-way and hedge position modes;
- position risk-limit updates;
- liquidation and ADL history.

## Safe update sequence

1. Read the latest account, position, and contract risk tier.
2. Check whether open orders or hedge-side positions block the change.
3. Submit the requested configuration update.
4. Read the position again and confirm the applied mode or leverage.
5. Recalculate displayed liquidation and margin values from the returned authoritative state.

<Warning>
  Reducing isolated margin or increasing leverage may materially increase liquidation risk. Show the new liquidation estimate and require explicit user confirmation.
</Warning>
