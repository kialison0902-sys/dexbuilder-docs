## Mark price

The mark price is designed to reduce the effect of short-lived price dislocations on unrealized PnL and liquidation. It is distinct from the last traded price and the external index price.

Use the mark price returned by DexBuilder for margin and liquidation displays. Do not recreate it from incomplete market data.

## Funding rate

Funding is a periodic transfer between long and short accounts that helps keep the perpetual market close to its index. A positive or negative market rate indicates the direction of the transfer for the applicable interval.

`text
funding payment = position notional × applicable funding rate
`

The exact sign, reference price, cap, floor, and settlement interval must come from the market and account fields returned by the API.

## Reconciliation

Keep these records separate:

- predicted or current market funding rate;
- historical market funding rate;
- account-specific funding payment;
- funding-related ledger entry.

<Note>
  A historical market rate does not prove that a particular account paid or received that amount. Use the account funding-payment record for user statements and reconciliation.
</Note>
