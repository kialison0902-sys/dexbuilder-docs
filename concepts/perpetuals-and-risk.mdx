---
title: "Perpetuals, funding, and risk"
description: "Understand margin, funding, liquidation, insurance, and ADL before integrating perpetuals."
---

Perpetual contracts do not expire. Their risk state changes continuously with price, leverage, collateral, funding, and open orders.

## Prices have different jobs

| Price | Primary use |
|---|---|
| Last price | Most recent trade and market display |
| Index price | External reference price |
| Mark price | Unrealized PnL, margin, and liquidation calculations |

Always use the authoritative fields returned by the API. Do not substitute last price for mark or index price.

## Funding

The market funding rate is a market-level value. The user's funding payment is an account-ledger event calculated from the applicable position and settlement interval. Reconcile the two separately.

## Liquidation and ADL

When maintenance margin is no longer satisfied, some or all of a position may be liquidated. If the liquidation engine and insurance fund cannot absorb the loss, auto-deleveraging may reduce profitable opposing positions according to protocol rules.

<Warning>
  Show margin mode, leverage, liquidation estimate, mark price, and risk warnings before any position-risk change. API clients must treat returned position and ledger state as authoritative.
</Warning>
