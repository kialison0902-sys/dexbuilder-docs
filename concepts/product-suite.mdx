DexBuilder provides a common foundation for multiple decentralized market products. A project can select the product model that fits its users while retaining a consistent integration approach for accounts, assets, data, and operations.

## Perpetual DEX

The perpetual DEX product is designed for continuous order-book trading without a fixed expiry. It supports the workflows expected by active and professional traders:

- contract metadata, order books, trades, candlesticks, tickers, and funding-rate history;
- limit, market, reduce-only, post-only, fill-or-kill, batch, and level-based BBO order workflows;
- one-way and hedge position modes;
- cross and isolated margin modes;
- leverage, position margin, exposure tiers, mark price, funding, liquidation, and ADL records;
- account state, account-book history, trading fees, fills, and position-close history.

## Prediction markets

Prediction markets organize trading around a clearly defined question and a set of possible outcomes. A complete market experience presents the market rules, trading state, outcome instruments, key dates, and resolution result in a form users can verify before trading.

Project teams use DexBuilder to bring prediction-market products into their own brand and user experience, alongside the account, asset, and operational workflows used across the platform.

## Event contracts

Event contracts are outcome-based contracts linked to a defined event. Each contract is presented with explicit terms, lifecycle states, trading availability, and settlement conditions so users can understand what determines the final result.

Event contracts extend the range of markets a Builder can offer while using the same product principles: clear specifications, consistent user state, traceable account records, and deterministic settlement outcomes.

## Shared product principles

| Principle | What users should expect |
|---|---|
| Clear market specification | Product terms, market state, supported actions, and settlement conditions are visible before an action is submitted |
| Consistent account state | Orders, positions or outcome exposure, balances, fees, and history are presented through a coherent account experience |
| Explicit authorization | Authenticated and asset-moving actions follow documented credential and wallet-authorization rules |
| Traceable lifecycle | Applications can track an action from submission through execution, ledger updates, and final settlement |
| Builder control | Project teams own the frontend, branding, user journey, and permitted product configuration |
