---
title: "Settlement model"
description: "The five settlement concepts that integrations must keep separate."
---

DexBuilder documentation uses **settlement** as a family of processes, not as one generic operation.

| Process | What changes | Expected evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Trade clearing | Position, margin, realized PnL, and trade fee | Current Trading API data |
| Funding settlement | Funding payment between long and short accounts | Current account funding history |
| PnL settlement | PnL becomes part of the settled account balance | PnL-settlement history and resulting balance |
| Asset settlement | Deposit or withdrawal reaches finality | Asset history and chain transaction |
| Builder revenue settlement | Daily Builder fee revenue is reported | Daily fee-revenue response |

## Integration rule

Never use the market's historical funding rate as evidence that a specific user paid or received funding. Use the user's funding payment ledger.

Similarly, do not derive Builder revenue from a trade's total fee. Use the daily fee-revenue response as the authoritative reconciliation record.

<Warning>
  Read the settlement eligibility, resulting balance, and withdrawable balance returned by the API before requesting a wallet signature.
</Warning>
