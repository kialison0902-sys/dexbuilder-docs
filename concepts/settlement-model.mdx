---
title: "Settlement model"
description: "The five settlement concepts that integrations must keep separate."
---

DexBuilder documentation uses **settlement** as a family of processes, not as one generic operation.

| Process | What changes | Expected evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Trade clearing | Position, margin, realized PnL, and trade fee | Fill and ledger entries |
| Funding settlement | Funding payment between long and short accounts | Funding payment entry |
| PnL settlement | PnL becomes part of the settled account balance | Settlement record and ledger entries |
| Asset settlement | Deposit or withdrawal reaches finality | Internal status and chain transaction |
| Builder revenue settlement | Builder revenue becomes payable or paid | Revenue ledger and payout record |

## Integration rule

Never use the market's historical funding rate as evidence that a specific user paid or received funding. Use the user's funding payment ledger.

Similarly, never use a trade's total fee alone to calculate Builder revenue. Protocol fees, Builder fees, rebates, referrals, and adjustments must be individually reconcilable.

<Warning>
  Read the settlement eligibility, resulting balance, and withdrawable balance returned by the API before requesting a wallet signature.
</Warning>
