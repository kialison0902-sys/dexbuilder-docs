---
title: "Permissions and scopes"
description: "Apply least privilege to Trading Keys and Builder Admin credentials."
tag: "Preview"
---

## Trading Key scopes

- `account:read`
- `orders:read`
- `orders:write`
- `positions:read`
- `positions:write`
- `funds:read`

Asset withdrawal and settlement submission should still require the applicable wallet-authorized payload.

## Builder Admin scopes

- `builder:read`
- `builder:write`
- `users:read`
- `fees:read`
- `fees:write`
- `revenue:read`
- `analytics:read`
- `webhooks:write`
- `markets:read`
- `markets:write`

<Note>
  The final production scope names are subject to change while the Builder API remains Preview.
</Note>
