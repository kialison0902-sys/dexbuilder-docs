---
title: "Permissions and scopes"
description: "Apply least privilege to Trading Keys and Builder Admin credentials."
---

## Trading Key scopes

- `account:read`
- `orders:read`
- `orders:write`
- `positions:read`
- `positions:write`
- `funds:read`

Asset withdrawal and settlement submission should still require the applicable wallet-authorized payload.

## ◇ Builder administrator permissions

DexBuilder has not published Builder permission names. The final model must separately authorize user-data reads, fee administration, revenue reads, order-tag management, referrals, campaigns, and Builder-filtered streams. Do not implement the scope names from an earlier draft.

<Note>
  Use only the permission names returned by a published DexBuilder credential service. Reject unknown permissions and request the minimum required access.
</Note>
