## Revenue formula

The exact production formula depends on the commercial plan. A revenue record should nevertheless expose all components:

```text
gross user trading fee
− protocol base fee
− user maker rebate
− referral and campaign allocations
± adjustments
= net Builder revenue
```

## Required records

- summary by day, market, and asset;
- fill-level revenue ledger;
- referral and campaign deductions;
- payable, paid, and adjusted balances;
- payout destination, transaction, and status.

<Warning>
  Do not calculate production payouts from analytics aggregates. Use the final revenue and payout ledgers.
</Warning>
