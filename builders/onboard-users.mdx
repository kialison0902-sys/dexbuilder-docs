<Warning>The operations below are Orderly reference paths and are not callable DexBuilder endpoints yet.</Warning>

## Target registration sequence

1. check registration with `GET /v1/public/account`;
2. read the registration nonce from `GET /v1/registration_nonce`;
3. construct the exact domain-separated registration message defined by the final contract;
4. obtain the wallet owner's signature;
5. submit `POST /v1/register_account` with Builder attribution;
6. confirm the account with `GET /v1/get_account` and the attribution with `GET /v1/get_broker`;
7. create a revocable trading credential with the equivalent of `POST /v1/orderly_key`.

Orderly uses `broker_id` in legacy wire fields. DexBuilder calls the product role Builder, but engineering must decide whether the public field is `broker_id` or `builder_id` before release.
