Builder revenue reporting is based on the Builder's active fee tier, user-facing fee rates, and daily settlement records.


## Builder information

| Method and path | Description |
|---|---|
| `GET /v1/broker/broker_info` | Get Builder fee-tier information |

The response identifies the Broker ID, active fee tier, base maker and taker rates, and revenue-settlement configuration.

## Daily revenue settlement

| Method and path | Description |
|---|---|
| `GET /v1/broker/daily_fee_revenue` | Get daily Builder revenue settlement history |

Daily records include the settlement date, trading volume, user fees, applicable base fees, Builder revenue, settlement asset, status, and a stable record identifier.

## Reconciliation

Reconcile each daily revenue record against Builder volume and account fee settings. Treat the daily revenue settlement response as the authoritative Builder-revenue record.
