---
title: "Build a DEX on DexBuilder"
description: "The project-level integration for teams onboarding and monetizing their own users."
---

A Builder is a project that launches a trading experience on DexBuilder infrastructure and owns the user relationship, product experience, fee policy, and operating workflow for that project.

## Builder capabilities

- create projects and environments;
- register and attribute users;
- configure default and user-specific fees;
- query Builder-scoped users, volume, fees, and revenue;
- configure referrals, campaigns, and points as they become available;
- receive signed events through webhooks;
- operate branded domains and deployments through the Builder Console.

## Responsibility boundary

DexBuilder provides trading execution, margin rules, ledger, and settlement infrastructure. The Builder remains responsible for its frontend, user disclosures, regional access policy, operational monitoring, and support processes unless a separate commercial agreement says otherwise.
