<Warning>`◇` means DexBuilder development is required. These capabilities are not yet published as callable DexBuilder APIs.</Warning>

A Builder operates a branded perpetual DEX experience while attributing users and activity to its Builder identity.

## Orderly-aligned scope

- user registration and Builder attribution;
- per-user trading credentials;
- Builder-scoped user, account, order, trade, position, funding, transfer, and liquidation data;
- default and user-specific fee rates;
- public and authenticated volume statistics;
- daily fee-revenue settlement data;
- order attribution tags, referrals, campaigns, and Builder-filtered data streams.

Project CRUD, generic transaction construction, webhook management, and payout creation are not included in this parity baseline.
