Funds and settlement APIs connect the trading ledger to user-authorized on-chain movements.

## Integration sequence

```text
Discover chain and contracts
        ↓
Request quote and nonce
        ↓
Build wallet transaction or typed message
        ↓
User reviews and signs
        ↓
Submit and track status
        ↓
Reconcile account ledger and chain evidence
```

## Balance fields

Clients should keep these values separate:

- total balance;
- available balance;
- frozen or order margin;
- position margin;
- unsettled PnL;
- withdrawable balance.

<Warning>
  The Funds & Settlement API remains Preview until chain addresses, signing domains, finality rules, and the production balance model are published.
