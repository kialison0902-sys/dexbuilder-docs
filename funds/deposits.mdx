description: "Construct, submit, and track a collateral deposit."

---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discover support">
    Query the supported chain, collateral asset, token address, vault address, minimum amount, and deposit status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request a quote">
    Validate the amount and receive expected network fees and confirmation requirements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the transaction">
    Use the Transaction Builder to construct approval and deposit calls for the registered account and Builder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request wallet signature">
    Display chain, asset, amount, destination contract, and risk summary before signing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and confirm">
    Track both chain finality and internal account credit. A successful chain transaction alone does not prove the trading balance was credited.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Suggested states

`CREATED → AWAITING_SIGNATURE → SUBMITTED → PROCESSING → CONFIRMED`

Failures should distinguish `REJECTED`, `FAILED_RETRYABLE`, and `FAILED_FINAL`.
