---
title: "◇ Deposits"
description: "Deposit collateral through the configured Vault contract, following the Orderly model."
---

<Warning>DexBuilder has not published the Vault contract and deposit environment contract. This page describes the required Orderly-aligned flow.</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="◇ Discover the active chain configuration">
    Read the supported chain, collateral token, Vault address, decimals, limits, and deposit status from the published environment configuration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="◇ Approve collateral">
    Have the wallet approve the configured Vault to spend the exact collateral amount when allowance is insufficient.
  </Step>
  <Step title="◇ Read the deposit fee">
    Call the Vault's `getDepositFee` method with the inputs required by the final contract.
  </Step>
  <Step title="◇ Submit the deposit">
    Have the user call the Vault's `deposit` method. The wallet must display the network, token, amount, contract, and fee.
  </Step>
  <Step title="◇ Confirm account credit">
    Wait for on-chain finality and verify the platform asset history and account balance. A successful transaction alone does not prove internal credit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Orderly does not use generic `POST /v1/deposits` or `POST /v1/deposits/quote` resources for this standard flow; DexBuilder therefore does not document them.
