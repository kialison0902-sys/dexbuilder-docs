<Note>`◇` identifies a deposit capability that is not currently available on DexBuilder.</Note>

## Deposit flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Load network configuration">
    Read the collateral token, Vault address, token decimals, minimum amount, and network status for the active environment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve collateral">
    If allowance is insufficient, ask the wallet owner to approve the exact collateral amount for the configured Vault.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the deposit fee">
    Call `◇ Vault.getDepositFee` with the parameters defined by the deployed contract.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit the deposit">
    Ask the wallet owner to call `◇ Vault.deposit` and display the network, token, amount, Vault address, and fee before confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the account credit">
    Verify the transaction finality, asset-history record, and updated account balance.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Confirmation requirements

A successful chain transaction confirms contract execution; it does not by itself confirm that the trading account has been credited. Use the account's asset history and balance as the final integration checks.
