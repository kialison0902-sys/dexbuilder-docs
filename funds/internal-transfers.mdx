Internal transfers move collateral between eligible accounts without using a blockchain withdrawal. They are distinct from deposits, withdrawals, and PnL settlement.

## Target flow

1. Validate source and destination accounts, asset, amount, and transfer eligibility.
2. Request a nonce and fee quote.
3. Build a domain-separated wallet authorization containing both account IDs, `builder_id`, asset, amount, nonce, and expiration.
4. Submit the signed transfer with an `Idempotency-Key`.
5. Track the transfer to a terminal state.
6. Reconcile equal and opposite ledger entries by one `transfer_id`.

<Warning>
  Internal-transfer endpoints are not yet part of the public Preview specification. Do not simulate a transfer with withdrawal and deposit calls.
</Warning>
