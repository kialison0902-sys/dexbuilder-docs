---
title: "◇ Internal transfers"
description: "Move collateral between eligible accounts with Orderly-aligned authorization."
---

<Warning>The operations below are Orderly references, not published DexBuilder endpoints.</Warning>

## Target operations

| Orderly reference | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `GET /v1/transfer_nonce` | Get the transfer signing nonce |
| `POST /v1/internal_transfer` | Submit a signed internal transfer |
| `POST /v2/internal_transfer` | Submit the current transfer version |
| `GET /v1/internal_transfer_history` | Query transfer history |

DexBuilder must select the supported transfer version and document account eligibility, direction, asset rules, signing payload, precision, statuses, and history schema before release. Do not simulate an internal transfer with a withdrawal followed by a deposit.
