Internal transfers move collateral between eligible accounts on the platform. They are separate from deposits, withdrawals, and PnL settlement.


## Internal transfer API

| Method and path | Description |
|---|---|
| `GET /v1/transfer_nonce` | Get the internal-transfer nonce |
| `POST /v2/internal_transfer` | Submit an internal transfer |
| `GET /v1/internal_transfer_history` | Get internal-transfer history |

## Transfer flow

1. validate source account, destination account, asset, amount, and eligibility;
2. obtain the transfer nonce;
3. construct the signed request defined by the transfer API;
4. submit the transfer;
5. confirm the result in internal-transfer history for the affected accounts.

Use the documented internal-transfer API. Do not implement an internal transfer as a withdrawal followed by a deposit.
