Reconciliation uses the authoritative history for each workflow rather than a single generic record type.

## Evidence by workflow

| Workflow | Authoritative records |
|---|---|
| Trading | Orders, fills, positions, funding, account balance, and account book |
| Deposits and withdrawals | Asset history and the corresponding chain transaction |
| PnL settlement | PnL settlement history and the resulting account balance |
| Internal transfer | Internal-transfer history for the affected accounts |
| Builder fees | Account fee settings, Builder volume, and daily revenue settlement history |

## Daily controls

1. reconcile executed order quantity against fills;
2. reconcile position changes against fills;
3. reconcile balance changes against account-book records;
4. reconcile asset history against chain transactions;
5. reconcile account fee rates against charged trading fees;
6. reconcile Builder volume against daily revenue settlement records.

Store stable identifiers, timestamps, statuses, and source references for every reconciliation result.
