title: "PnL settlement"
description: "Settle account PnL into the applicable balance."
tag: "Preview"
---

PnL settlement is separate from closing a position. Closing creates realized PnL; settlement applies the platform's ledger and on-chain settlement model to the account balance.

## Proposed flow

1. Request the settlement nonce.
2. Request a settlement quote showing the affected PnL and resulting balance.
3. Build a wallet-signable message containing the Builder, account, chain, nonce, and expiration.
4. Submit the signed request with an idempotency key.
5. Track the settlement until terminal status.
6. Reconcile the settlement record against the account ledger.

<Warning>
  Whether unrealized PnL is eligible, whether another party can trigger settlement, and when settled PnL becomes withdrawable are protocol facts that must be confirmed before this flow leaves Preview.
</Warning>
